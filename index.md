<section id="work">
  <div class="wrap">
    <div class="section-head">
      <p class="eyebrow">Results</p>
      <h2>Four things I built, and what changed.</h2>
      <p>I did this work as a Solutions Architect at Mesh Bio and in an MBA consulting project.</p>
    </div>

    <div class="service-grid">
      <div class="service-card">
        <h3>Clinic data, cleaned in minutes</h3>
        <p class="card-result">An hour a file became 6-10 minutes, including human review.</p>
        <p><b>Problem.</b> More than thirty clinics sent CSV and Excel files with different column and package names, and a person cleaned each file by hand.</p>
        <p><b>Built.</b> An AI pipeline that turns messy files into clean, checked output. A human reviewer checks every file before it is submitted.</p>
        <p><b>Measured.</b> The first version got at least one attribute wrong in 70% of records. After the fix, 99% of the data went to the live database.</p>
        <p class="card-meta">Mesh Bio, Singapore &middot; LangGraph, Claude on Bedrock, Pydantic, ChromaDB</p>
      </div>

      <div class="service-card">
        <h3>Onboarding new labs and clinics</h3>
        <p class="card-result">Onboarding improved from 3-5 days to under 1 day.</p>
        <p><b>Problem.</b> Every new lab and clinic needed its own hardcoded Lambda, which meant copying code and testing incoming data against it.</p>
        <p><b>Built.</b> One pipeline driven by configuration, handling about 10,000 records an hour in Singapore. It is still in use across Hong Kong, the Philippines, Singapore, Malaysia and Indonesia.</p>
        <p class="card-meta">Mesh Bio, Singapore &middot; AWS Lambda, SQS, SNS, EventBridge Pipes, Step Functions</p>
      </div>

      <div class="service-card">
        <h3>A first overseas customer, and a team of three</h3>
        <p class="card-result">The customer became a lighthouse account with follow-on regional projects.</p>
        <p><b>Problem.</b> The first overseas customer had a deadline and its own way of working.</p>
        <p><b>Built.</b> I recommended an API-based workflow, scoped it to the deadline and led three weeks of on-site deployment. I later built and led a team of three solutions engineers, who took over and expanded the APIs and pipelines.</p>
        <p class="card-meta">Mesh Bio, Singapore &middot; Solutions Architect</p>
      </div>

      <div class="service-card">
        <h3>Portfolio monitoring</h3>
        <p class="card-result">Projected to improve monitoring time by about 45%.</p>
        <p><b>Problem.</b> An impact investor monitored its portfolio by hand.</p>
        <p><b>Built.</b> A five-stage monitoring workflow, designed with the client, with AI tools and agents built as proofs of concept and an MVP across all five stages, from data clean-up to reporting. I pitched the recommendations to the investor's leadership.</p>
        <p class="card-meta">Consulting project with four classmates, Rotterdam School of Management &middot; Gemini, Claude and Tally.so</p>
      </div>
    </div>
